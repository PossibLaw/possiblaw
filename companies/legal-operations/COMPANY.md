PossibLaw Legal Operations is a Paperclip Agent Companies package for operating legal-business work through delegated AI agents. The operator may be a law firm or an in-house legal team running its own AI-native practice — the package assumes neither, and both delegate matters the same way.

The initial package proves one vertical slice: an operator assigns an NDA matter to the Chief of Staff, the matter moves through Chief Counsel and Commercial Lead, and the NDA Drafter returns a draft work product in Paperclip.

The practice of law is regulated. To the extent an operator is practicing law with this package, the operator needs to involve a lawyer. Keep that note at matter intake and do not repeat boilerplate disclaimers in every deliverable.
