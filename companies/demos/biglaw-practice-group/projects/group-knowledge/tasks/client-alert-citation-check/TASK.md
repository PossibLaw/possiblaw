Citation check on a draft client-alert paragraph before publication. The draft cites three authorities, all synthetic and supplied for this demo: (1) Pellucid Software, Inc. v. Marrowgate Retail Corp., 412 Calv. App. 4th 219 (Calverton Ct. App. 2024), cited for the proposition that a checkbox plus hyperlinked terms creates enforceable assent; (2) Thornquist v. BrambleGoods LLC, 88 F. Supp. 9th 1042 (D. Calv. 2023), cited for browsewrap failing without conspicuous notice; and (3) Calverton Consumer Contracts Act § 14-302, cited for a statutory notice requirement.

Verify each citation against the source excerpts supplied with the matter: confirm the citation format, that the quoted language appears in the source, and that each source actually supports the stated proposition. Report each citation as verified, mismatched, or unsupported, with the exact discrepancy. Do not silently fix the paragraph — discrepancies go back to the drafting associate as operator follow-ups.
