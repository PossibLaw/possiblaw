Client Orion Photonics is granting an outbound license to Tessellate Manufacturing covering Orion's lidar emitter patents and related know-how. Deal terms from the engagement memo: exclusive license limited to the automotive lidar field of use, worldwide territory, five-year initial term with renewal by mutual agreement. Royalty structure supplied by the client: $250,000 upfront, a running royalty of 4% of net sales of licensed products, and a $150,000 annual minimum royalty starting in year two.

Draft the patent and know-how license on these terms, with the field-of-use definition tight enough that Orion keeps all non-automotive applications. Sublicensing rights, improvement ownership, and patent-prosecution control were not addressed in the memo — surface all three as operator follow-ups before the draft goes to the client.
