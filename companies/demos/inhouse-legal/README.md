# Meridian Robotics, Inc. — In-House Legal Demo

Meridian Robotics, Inc. is a fictional 800-person industrial robotics company. The demo persona is its five-person legal department, which supports the whole business: commercial contracts in the sales and procurement pipeline, a growing privacy program with EU exposure, employment support for People Operations, and corporate governance for the board. This profile shows an in-house team using the legal delegation chain (chief-of-staff → chief-counsel → practice leads → specialists) across four practice areas, with deliberately incomplete facts so agents surface operator follow-ups for counsel instead of guessing.

## Use at Launch

- Org name: `Meridian Robotics Legal`
- Mission: `Meridian Robotics Legal is the five-person in-house legal department of Meridian Robotics, Inc., an 800-person industrial robotics company. AI agents prepare first-pass contracts, intakes, and reviews; the legal team reviews and owns every position.`

## Run the Demo

All tasks import into the backlog assigned to `chief-of-staff`. Move tasks from backlog to todo to start them. Suggested first pair: `review-quantis-msa` (commercial review chain) and `laptop-incident-tabletop` (privacy intake chain). Expected routing per task:

- `review-quantis-msa` → chief-of-staff → chief-counsel → commercial-lead → contract-reviewer
- `draft-auriga-nda` → chief-of-staff → chief-counsel → commercial-lead → nda-drafter
- `hivemind-dpa-addendum` → chief-of-staff → chief-counsel → privacy-lead → privacy-dpa-drafter
- `laptop-incident-tabletop` → chief-of-staff → chief-counsel → privacy-lead → privacy-incident-triage
- `vp-manufacturing-offer` → chief-of-staff → chief-counsel → employment-lead → employment-offer-letter-drafter
- `remote-work-policy-review` → chief-of-staff → chief-counsel → employment-lead → employment-policy-reviewer
- `option-pool-board-consent` → chief-of-staff → chief-counsel → corporate-lead → corporate-entity-drafter
- `calverton-ai-act-intake` → chief-of-staff → chief-counsel → regulatory-lead → regulatory-change-monitor

Watch for placeholder discipline: the board consent should leave authority-sensitive numbers as placeholders, the DPA should leave transfer-mechanism details as marked gaps, and the incident tabletop should end in operator follow-ups, not concluded legal positions.

## Synthetic Data Notice

Every company, vendor, person, statute, incident, and figure in this profile is synthetic and exists only for demonstration. The State of Calverton and its AI Transparency Act are fictional. Do not reuse any of it as real legal work product. The practice of law is regulated; to the extent an operator is practicing law with this workflow, the operator needs to involve a lawyer.
