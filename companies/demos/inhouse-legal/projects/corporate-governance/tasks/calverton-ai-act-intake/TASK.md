Regulatory-change intake. The statute below is fictional and supplied by the operator for this demo. Operator-provided summary to structure: "The State of Calverton has enacted the Calverton AI Transparency Act, effective the first day of next year. Companies that deploy automated decision systems affecting Calverton residents must (1) publish an annual transparency report describing the categories of systems in use, (2) provide notice at the point of interaction when a consumer-facing decision is automated, and (3) register covered systems with the Calverton Office of Algorithmic Accountability within 90 days of deployment. Civil penalties up to $20,000 per violation; a private right of action is expressly excluded."

Structure this into the regulatory-change intake format: scope and applicability to Meridian Robotics, key obligations and dates, affected business functions, and recommended next actions. Whether Meridian's products fall within "automated decision systems" under the Act is the central open question — surface it as an operator follow-up for counsel rather than answering it.
