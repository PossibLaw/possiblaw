Quantis Components KK, a prospective supplier of precision actuators, sent over its standard master services agreement for Meridian Robotics to sign. Meridian is the customer. Review the MSA with particular attention to the indemnity provisions and the limitation-of-liability cap — the early read from procurement says the cap is set at fees paid in the prior three months, far below Meridian's exposure if a defective component reaches a customer production line.

Also check warranty scope, IP ownership of any custom tooling, governing law (the draft reportedly selects foreign law with offshore arbitration), and termination rights. The contract file itself has not been attached yet; flag the upload as an operator follow-up and prepare the review framework in the meantime.
