Tabletop exercise on a synthetic fact pattern: on the evening of the 14th, a Meridian Robotics HR business partner left a company laptop in a taxi in Rotterdam. The laptop was not encrypted and held spreadsheets with names, home addresses, salary data, and national ID numbers for approximately 240 EU-based employees. The device has not been recovered, and there is no evidence either way on whether anyone accessed it. IT disabled the user's credentials the following morning.

Run the data-incident intake on these facts: classify the incident, identify which regulatory regimes plausibly apply and what notification clocks they would start, and lay out immediate containment and assessment steps. Regime determinations and deadline commitments are expected to surface as operator follow-ups for counsel, not as concluded legal positions.
