Meridian Robotics is rolling out Hivemind CRM as a SaaS processor that will handle EU employee contact and HR data for the Rotterdam office. Draft a data processing addendum for the Hivemind CRM subscription: Meridian as controller, Hivemind as processor, processing limited to documented instructions, sub-processor change notification, breach notice within 72 hours of awareness, audit cooperation, and deletion or return of data on termination.

Use Standard Contractual Clause placeholders for the transfer mechanism — module selection and annex details should be left as clearly marked placeholders for counsel to complete, not filled in. The definitive categories of data subjects and the vendor's sub-processor list are pending from Hivemind; record both as operator follow-ups.
