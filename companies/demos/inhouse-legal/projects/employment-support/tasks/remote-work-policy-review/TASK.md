People Operations refreshed the remote-work section of the Meridian Robotics employee handbook and wants legal review before publication. The revised section covers eligibility by role, a three-day in-office expectation for hybrid roles, home-office equipment stipends, working-hours and overtime expectations for non-exempt remote staff, and data-security obligations on home networks.

Review the section for enforceability and consistency risks: overtime and hours-tracking language for non-exempt employees, expense-reimbursement obligations that vary by jurisdiction, and any conflict with the handbook's existing at-will and workplace-monitoring provisions. Jurisdiction-specific reimbursement rules should be flagged for counsel as operator follow-ups rather than resolved in-line.
