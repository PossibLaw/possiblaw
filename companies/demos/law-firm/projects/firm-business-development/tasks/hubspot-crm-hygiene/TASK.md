The partners returned from the (fictional) Gulf Coast Legal Tech Summit with roughly forty new contacts scanned into the firm's HubSpot instance, many with incomplete fields and several likely duplicates of existing records. Run a CRM hygiene pass: propose merges for duplicates, normalize company names and titles, tag conference contacts with the event source, and produce a short list of follow-up-worthy leads for the BD partner.

No outbound emails should be sent as part of this task — the output is a cleanup plan and a prioritized lead list for operator review before any changes are applied.
