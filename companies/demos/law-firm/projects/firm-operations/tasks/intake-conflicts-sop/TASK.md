Draft a standard operating procedure for new-client intake and conflicts checking at Harbor & Finch. The SOP should cover initial inquiry capture, conflicts search across current and former clients and adverse parties, the lateral-hire conflicts wrinkle, engagement letter issuance, and matter opening in the firm's systems.

Write it as a step-by-step checklist a new office manager could follow, with explicit decision points for when a lawyer must be pulled in. Note open policy questions (for example the firm's lookback window for former-client conflicts) as operator follow-ups rather than choosing a policy unilaterally.
