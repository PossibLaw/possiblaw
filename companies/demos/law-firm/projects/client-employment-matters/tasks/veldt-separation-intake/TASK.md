Client Veldt Media is separating its marketing director and asked the firm to prepare a separation agreement. Treat this matter as confidential. Proposed terms from the client: twelve weeks of severance paid on the regular payroll schedule, benefits-continuation language, mutual non-disparagement, and a release of claims.

Run intake before drafting: confirm the employee's age (release-timing requirements may differ), tenure, state of employment, any existing restrictive covenants, and whether equity or commissions are outstanding. Anything the client has not supplied should surface as an operator follow-up before the separation agreement is drafted.
