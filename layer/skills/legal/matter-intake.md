# Matter Intake

Use this checklist to identify what information has been provided and what is missing. You do not need to ask the operator for every item — note gaps and apply defaults where the task allows.

1. **Parties** — Identify all parties involved: full legal names, entity types (individual, LLC, corporation), and jurisdictions of formation or residence.
2. **Matter type** — Classify the matter: NDA, contract review, contract draft, litigation hold, employment issue, IP licensing, regulatory inquiry, corporate formation, etc.
3. **Purpose / background** — Summarise what the operator wants to achieve. What is the business context? What transaction or relationship does this matter relate to?
4. **Urgency** — Determine the deadline or urgency level: routine (3–5 business days), expedited (same/next business day), or emergency (immediate).
5. **Jurisdiction** — Identify the governing law jurisdiction and any venue or forum selection requirements mentioned.
6. **Counterparty details** — Note the counterparty's name, role, and any known sensitivity (competitor, regulated entity, government body).
7. **Scope constraints** — Are there dollar-value thresholds, term limits, or scope restrictions the operator has specified?
8. **Conflicts seed data** — List all party names, affiliates, and counsel involved, so a conflicts check can be run.
9. **Prior versions or related documents** — Has the operator provided an existing draft, template, or prior agreement to work from?
10. **Confidentiality level** — Is this matter confidential to the operator's client, or is it an internal matter?
11. **Special instructions** — Any formatting requirements, house-style preferences, or clauses to include or exclude.
12. **Responsible lawyer** — Identify the supervising lawyer or partner who must approve output before use.
13. **Missing information** — List any fields above that are absent. Note which have defaults that will be applied and which require operator confirmation before proceeding.
