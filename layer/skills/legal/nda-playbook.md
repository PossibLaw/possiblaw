# NDA Drafting Playbook

Follow these steps in order to produce a complete, professional NDA. Apply the listed defaults whenever specific instructions are absent.

1. **Determine NDA type** — Default to a **mutual** NDA (both parties may disclose confidential information). Use a one-way (unilateral) NDA only if explicitly requested. Clearly label the agreement type in the title.

2. **Identify parties** — Name the Disclosing Party and Receiving Party (or both parties as "Party A" and "Party B" for mutual NDAs). Include full legal entity names and state/country of formation. Use placeholder `[PARTY NAME]` if a name is missing.

3. **Define "Confidential Information"** — Use a broad, inclusive definition: all non-public information disclosed by one party to the other, in any form (written, oral, electronic, visual), that is marked as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature and circumstances of disclosure. Attach a schedule of specific categories if the operator provides them.

4. **Standard exclusions** — Confidential Information expressly excludes information that: (a) is or becomes publicly known through no breach by the Receiving Party; (b) was already known to the Receiving Party before disclosure, as evidenced by written records; (c) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information; (d) is received from a third party who had the right to disclose it; or (e) is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or court order (provided the Receiving Party gives prompt prior written notice to the Disclosing Party and cooperates with any protective order).

5. **Obligations of the Receiving Party** — The Receiving Party must: (a) hold Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) use it solely for the Permitted Purpose; (c) disclose it only to its Representatives who have a need to know and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in the NDA; and (d) apply at least the same care it uses to protect its own confidential information, but no less than reasonable care.

6. **Permitted Purpose** — Clearly state the purpose for which Confidential Information may be used. Default to "evaluation of a potential business relationship between the parties." Replace with operator-specified purpose if provided.

7. **Term** — Default NDA term is **two (2) years** from the Effective Date. Confidentiality obligations with respect to trade secrets continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law, notwithstanding expiration of the NDA term.

8. **Permitted disclosures** — The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to its employees, contractors, agents, advisors, and legal counsel ("Representatives") who: (a) need access for the Permitted Purpose; and (b) are bound by written confidentiality obligations no less protective than those herein. The Receiving Party is liable for any breach by its Representatives.

9. **Governing law and dispute resolution** — Default governing law: **State of Delaware, USA**. Disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware, and each party consents to personal jurisdiction there. Replace with operator-specified jurisdiction if provided.

10. **No assignment** — Neither party may assign its rights or delegate its obligations under the NDA without the prior written consent of the other party. Any purported assignment without consent is void.

11. **No implied license** — Nothing in the NDA grants either party any right, title, interest, or license in or to any Confidential Information beyond the limited right to use it for the Permitted Purpose. All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party.

12. **Return or destruction of materials** — Upon request or expiration/termination of the NDA, the Receiving Party must promptly return or certifiably destroy all Confidential Information (and all copies, notes, and extracts thereof) and confirm compliance in writing. The Receiving Party may retain one archival copy solely for legal compliance purposes.

13. **Equitable relief** — The parties acknowledge that a breach of this NDA may cause irreparable harm not adequately compensated by monetary damages, and that the Disclosing Party is entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable relief without bond or proof of actual damages, in addition to all other remedies at law or in equity.

14. **Integration and amendment** — This NDA constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, and understandings. It may not be amended except by a written instrument signed by authorised representatives of both parties.

15. **Severability and waiver** — If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, it will be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, and the remaining provisions continue in full force. Failure to enforce any right under this NDA is not a waiver of that right for future breaches.

16. **Signature block** — Include a signature block for each party with: legal entity name, signatory name (placeholder: `[NAME]`), title (placeholder: `[TITLE]`), date (placeholder: `[DATE]`), and address. For mutual NDAs, provide two identical blocks side by side or stacked.
